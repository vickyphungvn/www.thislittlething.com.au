Our story

A little about me

Hello, I’m Vicky, an interior designer with a love for beautiful details and the little things that make life special.

As a designer, I’ve always believed that even the smallest details can bring warmth, joy and meaning.

That’s how thislittlething began.

I wanted to create something small, sweet and thoughtful. Something that feels like a little hug, a little kindness, or a little reminder that someone cares.

Whether it’s a simple thank you, a celebration, or just a way to say “I’m thinking of you,” I hope each little creation helps express the feelings that words sometimes can’t.

Because sometimes, the littlest things mean the most.

Made with love, from me to you. ♡
